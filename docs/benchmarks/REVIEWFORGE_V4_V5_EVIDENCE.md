These are planning estimates, **not confirmed billing**. Provider dashboard receipts are needed for actual charges.

The sample is small, synthetic, and selected for review evaluation. Source inspection confirms the seven findings but this audit did not execute a new mutation or concurrency test. No claim is made about production recall, precision, security coverage, latency distribution, or replacement readiness. Historical V1/V2 incomplete attempt outcomes remain separate and unknown.
